# Deferred

One line each: what differs or is missing, where it was seen, and why it did not block. Revisit after the exit test.

- Data-frame input: `hgam.frontend.gam` takes a mapping of NumPy columns and a `Factor` type where polaris-re took a polars DataFrame (polars is not a core dependency). pandas/polars input waits for an optional-extra decision (M4 or packaging).
- Moved engine tests left in polaris-re (43 test functions): ones that call the conformance modules, the old `experience_gam_penalized` engine, R probes or `VerificationClaim` objects; the statsmodels binomial cross-check (statsmodels is not a dependency); and three regression tests that read polaris-re's synthetic-data JSON files, which `oracle/make_fixtures.R` did not produce.
- Top-level `hgam.gam` / `hgam.bam` remain stubs; the moved front end is `hgam.frontend.gam`. Wiring waits for the port to reach the exit test.
- The six ported functions name mgcv 1.9-1, the version they were read from; their fixtures (`oracle/make_fixtures.R` section 4) are tier 1 from mgcv 1.9-1 until CI's 1.9-4 oracle copies are committed.
- Docstrings in the moved code cite polaris-re ADR numbers (ADR-189 to ADR-265) and `docs/PLAN_*` files that live in polaris-re, not here.
- `compress_covariates` returns unique rows in lexicographic order; mgcv's `compress.df` shuffles them with `sample()`. Same cell partition, different labels. Waits for R's random number stream in M3 (PR #3 review).
- `initial_log10_lambda_from_xx_diag` trims each penalty block to the span from its first to its last non-zero column, while mgcv's `initial.sp` uses the whole stored block. The row and column means behind the `eps^0.8` threshold can therefore differ when a block has zero edge columns. This affects starting values only (PR #3 review).
- REML score: `GamFit.reml_score` differs from mgcv's `m$gcv.ubre` by a normalising constant (782.5 vs 2525.5 on `gam_binomial_cloglog_ti_reml`, 227.0 vs 1087.8 on `gam_poisson_offset_reml`); smoothing parameters agree to 1e-8 relative on the ti fixture, 7e-5 on the Poisson and 4e-4 on `gam_binomial_cloglog_knots_reml`, so the optimum is the same (corrected after the PR #5 review). Not a key result.
- Standard errors of the linear predictor on `gam_binomial_cloglog_ti_reml` differ by up to 8.6e-4 relative (gate 1%); on the Poisson fixture by 6e-6. Likely the weights at which `Vp` is formed for a non-canonical link. Inside the gate.
- `knots=` names that no cr smooth or ti margin uses are refused; mgcv ignores them. Knots reaching a `tp` or `re` term are refused (not ported).
- cr extrapolation outside supplied knots: `_cr_basis_raw` extrapolates linearly as `crspl` does (checked by the PR #6 review), but no fixture holds data outside the supplied knots to mgcv; `gam_binomial_cloglog_knots_reml` keeps all data inside them.
- Smoothing parameters on `gam_binomial_cloglog_sz_reml` (`10**fit.log_lambda`, the engine's log10 scale): sz levels b and c agree with mgcv to 0.1% (384 vs 385, 5582 vs 5579); level a is 0.92 vs 2.42 and the near-linear s(x) 1.46e7 vs 3.96e7 (both a factor of about 2.7, s(x) on a flat REML ridge). eta (1.0e-4), edf (2.1e-3) and se (5.8e-4 relative) meet their gates. Not a key result. (Corrected after the PR #7 review: the first version read log10 values as natural logs.)
- Per-term edf split between a factor and its sz smooths (`f + s(f, x, bs="sz")`, two sz smooths of one factor; section 8, `tests/test_rank_deficient_sz.py`). eta, se, structure, the other terms' edf and the summed edf of the involved terms meet their gates (8a: f + s(f,x) 7.6228 vs 7.6207; 8b: f + s(f,x) + s(f,z) 4.969 vs 4.968), but the split differs (8a: f 0 / sz 7.62 vs mgcv 1 / 6.62; 8b: s(f,x) 3.60, s(f,z) 1.37 vs mgcv 2.60, 2.37). mgcv's split follows which directions `gdiPK` (gdi.c) drops by pivoted QR in the `totalPenaltySpace` basis at the working weights. Measured in R on 8a: that null-space basis is an arbitrary rotation inside a degenerate eigenspace (R's and scipy's dsyevr bases differ), and under 200 random rotations of it mgcv's own rule keeps both f columns 161 times and drops one 39 times; at prior instead of working weights it changes again on 2 of 4 seeds. The split is not portable and is not gated: Jonathan decided on PR #8 that the involved terms are gated on their summed edf (0.05 absolute or 1%, whichever is larger) and the split is reported as not identified.
- Smoothing parameters on `gam_binomial_cloglog_sz_select_reml` (section 9): the five per-level and null-space sp agree with mgcv to 0.002 in log10; the near-linear s(x)'s own penalty is log10 7.29 vs 8.16 (a flat REML ridge, as in section 7). eta, edf and se meet their gates. Not a key result.
- `null_space_penalty` (`gam_select_penalty.py`) cuts the null space at `np.linalg.matrix_rank`'s tolerance (about p * eps * max), mgcv's `smoothCon` at max * eps^0.66; same null dimension on every fixture (gap 2e-3 vs 4e-17), so not changed. Port mgcv's rule, add its counterpart line and fix the "never from mgcv's R source" docstring when the module is next touched (PR #10 review nit).
- The outer search's fixed lower bound log10(lambda) = -2 refuses fits where mgcv goes lower: a toy gaussian `s(x, bs="cr", k=5) + s(f, x, bs="sz", k=5, ...)` with a strong level curve gives mgcv log10 sp -2.99 for one level. hgam raises rather than approximates. Revisit if a fixture or the exit model hits it.
- Linear predictor on `gam_binomial_cloglog_by_numeric_reml` (section 10b, `s(x) + s(x, by=z)`) against mgcv's default fit: 1.24e-3 (gate 1e-3). mgcv's default newton stops short of its own REML optimum on the by smooth's flat ridge: log10 sp 6.248 vs 6.306 at `conv.tol = 1e-12`, REML 2441.46481 vs 2441.46465, and the two mgcv fits differ by 1.4e-3 in eta. Gated instead against the tight fit (Jonathan's decision on PR #12, option B): eta 1.9e-4, se 9.7e-4 relative, edf 3.5e-3. Not a key-result miss.
- A constant numeric `by` (mgcv keeps the sum-to-zero constraint) is refused, not ported.
- Smoothing parameter of the near-linear s(x) on `gam_binomial_cloglog_f_sz_select_reml` (section 11a): log10 7.40 vs mgcv's 7.83 (a flat REML ridge, as in sections 7 and 9); the other five sp agree to 0.001. eta (8e-5), se (3.6e-4) and edf (8.5e-4) meet their gates. Not a key result.
- mgcv's convergence message on section 11b depends on the data round trip: the fixture script's fit reports "full convergence", while the same model refitted from the fixture's JSON data reports "step failed", with eta equal to 1.9e-12. Not a key result.
- `gam(method="fREML", discrete=True)` (section 12): the ti term's two smoothing parameters differ from mgcv's discrete bam by about 10% (12a: 88.0 and 10.0 vs 79.4 and 9.03), likely because `discretise_frame` takes the sum-to-zero constraints over the data rows and mgcv over its padded frame of unique values (`discrete.mf`, `gam.setup` on `mf0`). eta 3.7e-4, se 1.2e-3 relative, edf 7e-3 meet their gates. Not a key result.
- `GamFit.predict` on new data from a discrete fit evaluates the basis at the covariates given; mgcv's `predict.bam` re-runs `discrete.mf` on new data (binning on that data's own range). The fitted eta and se at the model frame match (section 12). Not yet needed by the exit test.
- mgcv's `bam` at `discrete = FALSE` warns "algorithm did not converge" (`bgam.fit`) on the `exact` references of 12d and 13a. Those references are informational (no gate uses them; hgam has no `discrete = FALSE` bam path).
